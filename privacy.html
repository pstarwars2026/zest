Privacy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Zest is a cookbook companion to Techie Traveler Foodie, published by pstarwars2026. This policy describes the app and this product website. Zest is currently in development.

Saved on your device

Zest does not require a Zest account. Favourites, meal plans, shopping-list selections, cooking progress and saved outings are stored locally. They do not automatically sync through a Zest server. Backups are saved to the location you choose; a cloud storage provider may process a backup if you save it there.

Optional chef voice

Starting a Gemini voice session sends microphone audio and context about the selected recipe, servings and cooking step directly to Google. Google returns generated speech and text. The session stops when you end it or move the app to the background. Zest does not save audio recordings. Displayed transcripts are transient and excluded from recipe backups.

Your Gemini API key is stored in this device’s Keychain with device-only accessibility. It is sent directly to Google to authorize voice access; it is not sent to a Zest-operated server. Remove it in Zest Settings. Google’s processing, retention, API terms and account billing apply to your requests; see Gemini API terms and Google privacy policy.

YouTube videos

Opening the video player connects to YouTube. The embedded player may use cookies or similar storage, receive network and device information, process playback activity and show advertising under Google’s policies. Choosing Open in YouTube uses your browser or YouTube app. Zest does not receive your YouTube account password or viewing history from YouTube.

Recipe updates and this website

The planned recipe feed and artwork are static files hosted through GitHub Pages. Requests may expose ordinary connection data, such as your IP address and request headers, to the hosting provider. The app does not attach your saved collection, voice key or purchase receipt to recipe-file requests. This website has no advertising scripts, analytics scripts or embedded video player. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Places and purchases

Place searches use Apple Maps services with the search terms you enter. The current app does not request your GPS location. Selecting a venue website or opening Maps connects to that service. Apple processes in-app purchases and restores; Zest uses StoreKit’s verified purchase status to unlock Pro. Zest does not receive your payment-card number.

Permissions and choices

The microphone is used for a voice session you start. Notifications are optional and used for cooking timers. File access is limited to files you select for backup and restore. You can deny permissions in device settings and continue using the other cookbook features.

Remove saved items in the app, remove the Gemini key in Settings, and delete exported backups from their chosen storage locations when no longer wanted. Local app data follows the operating system’s app-removal and backup behavior; a Keychain item can persist after app removal, so remove the key in Settings first.

Support

Support issues on GitHub are public and processed by GitHub. Do not include secrets, receipts, health information or private backups. For questions about this policy, use the support page.
