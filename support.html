A little help

Zest support

Recipes, purchases and your saved kitchen.

Zest is currently in development. These pages describe the intended launch experience; the app is not yet available on the App Store.

Where are the videos?

Open a recipe and choose Watch the video. The official YouTube player needs an internet connection and may show ads. You can also choose Open in YouTube. Public videos are free, including videos linked to Pro recipes.

What does Pro include?

Pro unlocks the growing reviewed cookbook, more meal plans and outings, and optional Gemini chef voice. It is planned as a US$1.99 one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription. Apple shows the actual local price before you buy. New batches arrive through periodic App Store updates. Preloaded recipes become available on their planned video release dates. Keep Zest updated to receive later batches; you do not need to buy Pro again.

How do I restore Pro?

Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase. In Zest, open Settings → Manage purchase → Restore Purchases, or open Zest Pro and choose Restore Purchases. Internet access is required. Restoring Pro does not transfer saved meals or favourites between devices.

How does chef voice work?

With Pro, save your own Gemini API key in Settings. Open a recipe, choose Cook now, then open the chef voice control and allow microphone access when asked. Voice needs internet and an API account with access to the supported Live model. Google usage charges and limits are separate from the Zest purchase.

Your key is stored in this device’s Keychain. Choose Remove key in Settings to delete it. A Google consumer subscription does not by itself establish API access. Never include API keys in a support request.

Can I use recipes offline?

The included recipe text and images work offline. App updates, YouTube videos, voice, place searches and purchase operations need internet. An upcoming preview shows the next preloaded recipe; the full recipe appears on its planned release date. Scheduled dates follow your device’s date, and a channel schedule may occasionally change.

How do I move my collection?

In Settings, choose Save a backup. Keep that file somewhere you control, then choose Restore a backup on the other device. Restoring replaces that device’s saved collection. API keys and voice transcripts are excluded. Zest does not automatically sync your collection between devices.

Why did a timer not alert me?

Allow Zest notifications in your device settings. Focus modes and notification settings can silence alerts. Keep an eye on your food; app timers are a convenience, not a substitute for checking doneness.

Are the recipes and pictures kitchen-tested?

The cookbook is editorially reviewed for measurements, clear steps and matching channel material. We do not claim physical kitchen testing. Artwork is illustrative. Cooking times vary by equipment and portions; check doneness, ingredient labels and any dietary restrictions.

Still need help?

Send a support request

Support requests are public and require a GitHub account. Include your device, app version and what happened. Do not post API keys, passwords, receipts, personal contact details or private recipe backups.

Purchase refunds are handled by Apple.
