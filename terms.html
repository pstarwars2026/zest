Zest: Daily Recipes is published by Parashar Shah (pstarwars2026). It is a cookbook companion to Techie Traveler Foodie for adults aged 18 and older. The app has been submitted to Apple and is awaiting review.

Cooking with Zest

Zest includes recipe instructions, planning tools and videos. Recipes are editorially reviewed and artwork is illustrative. Check ingredient labels, dietary suitability and cooking doneness. Optional AI chef responses can be mistaken; use your judgment when cooking.

Recipe videos

Zest uses YouTube API Services for its embedded player. By using Zest, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy explains Google’s handling of information. Please also read Zest’s privacy policy.

Zest Pro

Pro is an optional one-time in-app purchase at the price displayed by Apple. It unlocks the included Pro cookbook, additional planning features and optional chef voice. New recipe batches arrive through app updates. Preloaded recipes appear on planned dates; schedules may change. Public YouTube videos remain free. Apple handles payments, restoration and refund requests.

Optional Gemini chef

Voice requires your own eligible Gemini API account and key. Google’s Gemini API terms, fees, limits and supported regions apply separately. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, use an API project with active Cloud billing. Pro does not pay for API usage. The cookbook does not require voice or an API key.

Your collection

Saved recipes and plans stay on your device. Keep a backup if you want to preserve or transfer them. Backups do not contain your API key. Videos, voice, place searches, purchases and app updates need internet.
