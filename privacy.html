Privacy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Zest is a cookbook companion to Techie Traveler Foodie, published by pstarwars2026. This policy describes the app and this product website. Zest is currently in development.

Adult audience and your choice

Zest is for adults 18 and older. Before entering the app, you confirm that you are an adult and agree to this policy and the terms. The agreement version and adult confirmation stay on this device, separate from recipe backups. Zest does not ask for your date of birth.

Saved on your device

Zest does not require a Zest account. Favourites, meal plans, shopping-list selections, cooking progress and saved outings are stored locally. They do not automatically sync through a Zest server. Backups are saved to the location you choose; a cloud storage provider may process a backup if you save it there.

Optional chef voice

Starting a Gemini voice session sends microphone audio and context about the selected recipe, servings and cooking step directly to Google. Google returns generated speech and text. The session stops when you end it or move the app to the background. Zest does not save audio recordings. Displayed transcripts are transient and excluded from recipe backups.

Your Gemini API key is stored in this device’s Keychain with device-only accessibility. It is sent directly to Google to authorize voice access; it is not sent to a Zest-operated server. Remove it in Zest Settings. Google’s processing and API billing apply separately. For unpaid API services, Google may use requests and responses to improve its products and models, and human reviewers may process them. For paid API services, Google says it does not use them for product improvement, but retains them for a limited period for safety, security and legal purposes. API authentication and usage information may also be retained. Avoid sharing private or sensitive information with the chef. See Gemini API terms and Google privacy policy.

YouTube videos

Opening the video player connects to YouTube using its privacy-enhanced embed mode. Zest uses a temporary website data store for each player and does not start playback automatically. YouTube says views in this mode do not personalize the viewer’s YouTube experience or advertising outside the embed, and any ads shown in the player are non-personalized. This is not a no-data-collection mode: YouTube can still receive network and device information and process playback and advertising activity under Google’s policies. See YouTube’s embed privacy information. Choosing Open in YouTube uses your browser or YouTube app. Zest does not receive your YouTube account password or viewing history from YouTube.

Provider identifiers and diagnostics

Google may retain account or project identifiers, browser or device identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, service activity, ad interactions and diagnostic information such as errors and performance metrics. These support service operation, security, analytics and non-personalized advertising. Zest does not claim this provider data is anonymous. Zest does not request your advertising identifier or GPS location.

Recipe updates and this website

Recipes and artwork are bundled with the app. New batches arrive through App Store updates. Completed, scheduled recipes may be included ahead of time and become visible on their planned release dates using your device’s date. Zest does not contact a recipe server to unlock them. This product website is hosted through GitHub Pages; visiting it may expose ordinary connection data, such as your IP address and request headers, to GitHub. This website has no advertising scripts, analytics scripts or embedded video player. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Places and purchases

Place searches use Apple Maps services with the search terms you enter. The current app does not request your GPS location. Selecting a venue website or opening Maps connects to that service. Apple processes in-app purchases and restores; Zest uses StoreKit’s verified purchase status to unlock Pro. Zest does not receive your payment-card number.

Permissions and choices

The microphone is used for a voice session you start. Notifications are optional and used for cooking timers. File access is limited to files you select for backup and restore. You can deny permissions in device settings and continue using the other cookbook features.

Remove saved items in the app, remove the Gemini key in Settings, and delete exported backups from their chosen storage locations when no longer wanted. Local app data follows the operating system’s app-removal and backup behavior; a Keychain item can persist after app removal, so remove the key in Settings first.

Support

Support issues on GitHub are public and processed by GitHub. Do not include secrets, receipts, health information or private backups. For questions about this policy, use the support page.
